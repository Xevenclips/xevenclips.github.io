Privacy Policy

Xevenclips Media · Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Who we are

Xeven Autopilot ("the app") is a private tool operated by Xevenclips Media to manage YouTube channels that we own or manage. It runs on our own computer and is not offered to the general public.

2. What data the app accesses

When a channel owner signs in with Google and clicks "Allow", the app receives read-only access through these Google permissions:

  • YouTube Data API (youtube.readonly) — the channel's name and ID, and its list of videos with titles, publish dates and public statistics.
  • YouTube Analytics API (yt-analytics.readonly) — performance reports for that channel, such as views, impressions, click-through rate, watch time and audience retention.
The app does not request permission to upload, edit or delete videos, comments or channel settings.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to show the channel owner and their team how the channel's videos are performing, and to suggest improvements such as a new title or thumbnail. Any change to a video is made only after a person on the team approves it.

4. Where the data is stored

Sign-in tokens and downloaded reports are stored only on our own computer. They are not sent to any other server operated by us, and we do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone. The data is not used for advertising.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Xeven Autopilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. How to remove access

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once access is removed, the app can no longer read the channel's data. To ask us to delete stored reports, contact us at the email below.
